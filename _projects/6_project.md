**May 2022 – May 2023** · Machine Learning Intern — **Pazhou Lab**, Guangzhou, China

Built an end-to-end **sleep disorder detection system** over physiological signal data as part of a national competition track.

- Ranked **5th out of 171 teams nationally**, securing a **$10,000 award**.
- Improved model accuracy from **55% → 59%** across **7 datasets**, while achieving a **110× runtime speedup** through pipeline and inference optimization.

This work sits alongside my graduate research on interpretable graph neural networks for physiological signals, which produced publications at **IEEE BIBM 2023** and in **IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing**.
